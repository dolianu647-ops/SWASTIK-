SWASTIK Play Store notes

1. Generate a signed AAB from this project.
2. Create the SWASTIK app in Google Play Console.
3. Complete the store listing, app content, data-safety and privacy-policy sections.
4. Upload the AAB to a testing track first.
5. If your Google Play personal developer account is subject to the current testing rule, you must satisfy Google's required testing period before production access.

Do not publish passwords, private keys, signing keystore files, or database files in GitHub.
